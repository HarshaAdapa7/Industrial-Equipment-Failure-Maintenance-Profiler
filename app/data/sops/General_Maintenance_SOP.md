# General Preventive Maintenance SOP for CNC Milling Equipment

## 1. Overview
Predictive and preventive routine maintenance schedule for CNC Milling assets to maximize machine uptime, extend component life, and minimize unexpected breakdown risk.

## 2. Daily Checklist
- Clean chips from machine way covers and tool magazine.
- Check hydraulic oil level and pneumatic pressure gauge (6.0 bar nominal).
- Check flood coolant level and refractometer concentration (5% to 8%).
- Verify auto-lube pump reservoir and grease lines.

## 3. Weekly Maintenance
- Clean air filters on spindle chiller and electronics cabinet.
- Inspect spindle taper and clean with wiper tool.
- Perform axis lubrication purge.

## 4. Monthly & Quarterly Inspection
- Check ball screw backlash on X, Y, and Z axes using dial indicator.
- Verify machine leveling and foundation torque.
- Perform thermographic inspection of electrical panel contactors.
